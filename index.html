Waterloo Private Capital is the premier student-led club at the University of Waterloo focused on private capital. Our members provide advisory services to private equity firms and institutional investors.

Mission

Waterloo Private Capital is a student club at the University of Waterloo. Our members support private equity firms and institutional investors with industry research and transaction analysis.

WPC gives students hands-on experience in private capital and a strong start to a career in finance. Our alumni have gone on to leading firms across Canada and the United States. We also run panels and workshops that are open to every Waterloo student.

Services

How our members support clients

I

Industry Research

Primary and secondary research on growth, profitability, competitive landscape, emerging trends and sources of disruption.

II

Transaction Analysis

Due diligence and valuation support on live deals, including comparable company and precedent transaction analysis.

III

Special Assignments

Deal sourcing, target identification, investment ideas and market sizing, scoped to each client's needs.

Community

Supporting the Waterloo community

We share what we learn with students across campus. Our members host panels, workshops and networking sessions that are open to all University of Waterloo students.
